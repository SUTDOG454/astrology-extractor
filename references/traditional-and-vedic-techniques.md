# Traditional & Vedic Techniques Checklist

Extract when present in the source:

## Hellenistic / Traditional

- Sect (day/night), luminaries, and related techniques
- Essential dignities (domicile, exaltation, triplicity, terms, faces)
- Accidental dignities and debilities
- Dispositors, reception, mutual reception, almuten
- Lots / Arabic Parts beyond Fortune and Spirit
- Profections, zodiacal releasing, primary directions, firdaria
- Time-lords and annual methods

## Vedic / Jyotish

- Rasi chart and relevant Vargas (Navamsa D9 especially)
- Nakshatras and pada
- Dashas (Vimshottari and others named)
- Yogas (Raja, Dhana, etc.) when listed
- Ayanamsa used (Lahiri, Raman, KP, etc.)
- Ashtakavarga, Shadbala, or other quantitative systems if present

## Integration with KP

When KP data appears, prefer the dedicated KP reference and scripts; still capture any traditional or Vedic context the author supplies.
